Convention Cards From Play

Each card describes the system a model actually bids, learned from its own four-seat play with nobody vulnerable; WBridge5's card is fitted the same way on its training-split deals. A convention is a guideline, not a law: the ranges describe the hands a bid is usually made with, and red marks an entry whose range has moved away from WBridge5's, not a single odd bid.

All side by side

Per entry, the 5th–95th percentile of the hands that made the call: their HCP, or the length they held in the suit the entry is about. "Key suit" gives, for the entry's most common bid and auction, the suit whose mean length is furthest from an average hand's 3.25 cards. Click a column heading to open that model's card.

How a range is read

"15–17" means 90% of the hands this model opened 1NT with held 15 to 17 HCP. "Held" gives the same 5th–95th percentile for the length of the suit the entry is about (the bid suit, partner's major, their suit...), with its median and the full distribution as bars; pooled entries such as 1♥/1♠ also give it per call. The share after a feature such as "balanced 90%" is how many of those hands had it. "Frequency" is how often the call is chosen among all decisions in that situation.

Suit bars

One panel per bid and auction ("X after 1♦" is a double of a 1♦ opening). Each bar is the mean length held in that suit on a 0–9 scale, the whisker the 5th–95th percentile, the dark tick the median, and the dashed line the 3.25 cards of an average hand. The key suit, in blue, is the one furthest from that line: long for a suit the bid shows, short for one it denies. When every suit is within half a card of it, no suit is marked.

When an entry is red

An entry is red when either end of its HCP range moves 2 or more points from WBridge5's, either end of the length range held moves 2 or more cards or its median moves 1 or more, its feature share moves 10 or more points, or the call is made at least 1.5 times as often (or at most two thirds as often). One-point moves are within sampling noise at these counts.

Fit columns

"Own card fits" is the share of held-out decisions that the model's own decision-tree card predicts. "WBridge5's card fits" applies WBridge5's tree to the same decisions. "Auctions played differently" counts auctions where the model's own card beats WBridge5's by 10 or more points.

Source

The cards are evaluation/convention_cards_from_play.py's <model>.json files in this folder; this page is written by evaluation/convention_card_figures.py, evaluation/convention_card_classic.py draws each card as a one-page printed card (classic/), and evaluation/convention_card_ebu_form.py fills in the English Bridge Union's standard card from the model's play (ebu/).
